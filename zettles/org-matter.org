#+TITLE: Org matter

Org matter is the collection of Org files that poslib manages.

It holds tasks, project files and review items. It follows strict
conventions, because poslib reads and rewrites it. One sequence of TODO
keywords applies to all of it. It is the
[[id:f4e86452-48ad-4ffb-8218-d2491c92a3ca][canon]] of a
[[id:97a43a8f-e4e2-4b6f-9c08-b11a0455eac5][responsibility]], and part of
the canon of every [[id:e6a6108d-d2c0-4999-a9fa-c759e0ef9b23][project]].
The [[id:93d0542c-ef28-4af2-bff4-5f16e3f87021][corpus]] is the Org matter
that poslib reads under one directory. A
[[id:4942c91a-4024-44d5-b549-41a105415fdd][garden]] is a hierarchy of it.
A [[id:cea827dc-7a1e-4da8-8c15-c32be34b5b54][kind of canon]] is canon that
is not Org matter.
