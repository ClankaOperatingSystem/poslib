#+TITLE: boundary

A boundary is a path that a
[[id:a4295636-b585-4062-92d6-90109d9dfc2d][configuration]] lists as a
child.

Everything at that path belongs to the child. The child is a
[[id:97a43a8f-e4e2-4b6f-9c08-b11a0455eac5][responsibility]], a
[[id:e6a6108d-d2c0-4999-a9fa-c759e0ef9b23][project]] or a
[[id:1afc8602-cf2b-469a-9813-ef7154f8b459][product]]. It can be a
directory in the same repository, or a separate repository mounted there.
A boundary is where a [[id:530709f8-639a-4701-851e-11b93907abd3][scope]]
ends. What lies past it is another scope, or a product. In a project, the
child can also be a
[[id:7442c287-5543-48e2-a20a-855af70fc35e][methodology]]. The
[[id:a5472c74-3ec5-482d-9e12-de172e55cb31][dependency rule]] stops links
from crossing it upwards.
