#+TITLE: dependency rule

The dependency rule states that a
[[id:530709f8-639a-4701-851e-11b93907abd3][scope]] refers only to what
lies inside it, and never to what contains it or sits beside it.[fn:1]

The dependency rule applies throughout a
[[id:4942c91a-4024-44d5-b549-41a105415fdd][garden]]: every
[[id:97a43a8f-e4e2-4b6f-9c08-b11a0455eac5][responsibility]] and
[[id:e6a6108d-d2c0-4999-a9fa-c759e0ef9b23][project]] in the
[[id:eda47489-3b75-4229-8c40-f74795fbca20][tree of responsibilities]] is a
scope under it, and a [[id:1afc8602-cf2b-469a-9813-ef7154f8b459][product]]
keeps its own rules. A
[[id:c587099a-d0f8-4c7a-a0b5-0b42ecc6daa8][boundary]], drawn by a
[[id:a4295636-b585-4062-92d6-90109d9dfc2d][configuration]], marks where
one scope ends and a contained scope begins, so the rule runs one way: a
container refers down into what it contains, and what is contained behaves
as though its container does not exist. The rule limits
[[id:f4e86452-48ad-4ffb-8218-d2491c92a3ca][canon]], which links to its own
scope's canon, to the scopes inside that one, and to its own
[[id:d9a04968-d3c5-4c68-8078-093ef619dd1d][archive]] by
[[id:cc5b47b1-e21a-4ea5-927c-13f5a558e6b5][ipfs link]].
[[id:d5a281ef-457a-44ad-97f7-92f46e207e31][Sealing]] applies it to each
[[id:efba3260-b37e-461a-aabe-b285799a4968][sealed item]]: a link from the
item to anything outside the archives of its scope is replaced by a link
to a [[id:0bca5ce7-5bdd-4a66-84d1-66c30c79caf7][rumour]], which records
that the thing was said to exist.

[fn:1] The name is Robert C. Martin's. His Dependency Rule says that
"source code dependencies can only point inwards":
[[https://blog.cleancoder.com/uncle-bob/2012/08/13/the-clean-architecture.html][The
Clean Architecture]], 2012, and the book /Clean Architecture/, 2017. See
[[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_C._Martin][Robert C. Martin]] on
Wikipedia, which also lists his /Clean Code/, 2009.
