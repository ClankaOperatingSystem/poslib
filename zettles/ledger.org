#+TITLE: ledger

A ledger is the ordered record of every seal made into one
[[id:d9a04968-d3c5-4c68-8078-093ef619dd1d][archive]].

Each archive has one ledger. It is a numbered series of files, and each
file is one [[id:8322059f-d15d-4dc2-86b2-27625b2c8b82][event]]. Each event names the one before it, so an event
cannot be removed or changed unnoticed. The ledger stays with its
[[id:530709f8-639a-4701-851e-11b93907abd3][scope]], even when a [[id:219a360a-81c8-4c47-8f9c-574e862ec12b][keeper]] holds the archive. The [[id:468d8a4f-eff3-427d-be75-a7922c939cfc][fold]]
of a ledger gives everything the archive should hold, without reading the
archive. A [[id:f3bfe249-83ff-45c3-ba76-d1f6460f97b8][checkpoint]] records where a ledger ended.
