#+TITLE: tree of responsibilities

A tree of responsibilities is a hierarchically organised collection of
personal responsibilities, represented as directories in a filesystem.

Each branch is a [[id:97a43a8f-e4e2-4b6f-9c08-b11a0455eac5][responsibility]]. A leaf is a [[id:1afc8602-cf2b-469a-9813-ef7154f8b459][product]], a
[[id:e6a6108d-d2c0-4999-a9fa-c759e0ef9b23][project]], or a responsibility with no projects or products. Each
responsibility has a [[id:a4295636-b585-4062-92d6-90109d9dfc2d][configuration]]: a file that lists the paths of
its children. Tools learn what kind of thing a directory is from that
file, not from the directory's name. One tree can span several
repositories, and each joins at a [[id:c587099a-d0f8-4c7a-a0b5-0b42ecc6daa8][boundary]]. A whole tree, or a part
of one, is called a [[id:4942c91a-4024-44d5-b549-41a105415fdd][garden]].
