#+TITLE: responsibility

A responsibility is an area of life or work that a person looks after and
that does not finish.

It is a directory in a [[id:eda47489-3b75-4229-8c40-f74795fbca20][tree of
responsibilities]]. It can hold other responsibilities, as well as a set
of [[id:e6a6108d-d2c0-4999-a9fa-c759e0ef9b23][projects]] that drive it
forwards, and a set of
[[id:1afc8602-cf2b-469a-9813-ef7154f8b459][products]] that belong to it.
Its [[id:a4295636-b585-4062-92d6-90109d9dfc2d][configuration]] says where
its projects are kept, and that marks it as a responsibility. Its tasks
and notes are [[id:f8f269fe-5e28-49dc-bc73-b9bae96edead][Org matter]]. It
is a [[id:530709f8-639a-4701-851e-11b93907abd3][scope]], so it has its own
[[id:f4e86452-48ad-4ffb-8218-d2491c92a3ca][canon]] and its own
[[id:d9a04968-d3c5-4c68-8078-093ef619dd1d][archive]]. Each child it holds
is behind a [[id:c587099a-d0f8-4c7a-a0b5-0b42ecc6daa8][boundary]].
