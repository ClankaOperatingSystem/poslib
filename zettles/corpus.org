#+TITLE: corpus

A corpus is the set of Org files that poslib reads under a directory.

It is every Org file under that directory, except those in excluded
directories. A [[id:a4295636-b585-4062-92d6-90109d9dfc2d][configuration]]
lists the exclusions. By default they are every
[[id:d9a04968-d3c5-4c68-8078-093ef619dd1d][archive]], every attic, and
every directory whose name starts with an underscore or a dot. Files
inside a [[id:1afc8602-cf2b-469a-9813-ef7154f8b459][product]] are not in
the corpus. poslib's commands for tasks and reviews read the corpus and
nothing else. What the corpus holds is
[[id:f8f269fe-5e28-49dc-bc73-b9bae96edead][Org matter]]. The files of a
[[id:7442c287-5543-48e2-a20a-855af70fc35e][methodology]] are not in the
corpus.
