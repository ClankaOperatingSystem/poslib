#+TITLE: archive

An archive is the part of a
[[id:530709f8-639a-4701-851e-11b93907abd3][scope]] that accumulates.

An archive is the complement of
[[id:f4e86452-48ad-4ffb-8218-d2491c92a3ca][canon]]. Canon evolves, and the
archive accumulates. An archive is made of
[[id:efba3260-b37e-461a-aabe-b285799a4968][sealed items]]. A sealed item
never changes, so an archive only grows. Things enter it by
[[id:d5a281ef-457a-44ad-97f7-92f46e207e31][sealing]] and in no other way.
Its [[id:1e668ccf-548f-4c26-ae57-ddb057d4c7a2][ledger]] records every
seal. Canon cites the archive by
[[id:cc5b47b1-e21a-4ea5-927c-13f5a558e6b5][ipfs link]], which is how
canon's provenance is traced. An archive is kept on disk beside its scope,
or by a [[id:219a360a-81c8-4c47-8f9c-574e862ec12b][keeper]]. The scope's
[[id:a4295636-b585-4062-92d6-90109d9dfc2d][configuration]] says which. The
[[id:90cd82cd-8947-4015-91b5-53335880dd64][recorded root]] is the id of
everything in the archive. A
[[id:f3bfe249-83ff-45c3-ba76-d1f6460f97b8][checkpoint]] shows if the
archive or its ledger has gone missing.
