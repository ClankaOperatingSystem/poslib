#+TITLE: product

A product is a directory in a
[[id:eda47489-3b75-4229-8c40-f74795fbca20][tree of responsibilities]] that
is kept under its own rules.

A product can be the subject of a
[[id:e6a6108d-d2c0-4999-a9fa-c759e0ef9b23][project]], i.e. a project
generally produces change to a product. The two are not the same thing,
and many people confuse them. The project is the work, and it ends. The
product is the thing worked on, and it stays. A product belongs to a
[[id:97a43a8f-e4e2-4b6f-9c08-b11a0455eac5][responsibility]], or to a
project that works on it. It is usually a repository of its own. It has no
poslib [[id:a4295636-b585-4062-92d6-90109d9dfc2d][configuration]], which
is how poslib tells it from a responsibility or a project. Its parent's
configuration declares it, and its path there is a
[[id:c587099a-d0f8-4c7a-a0b5-0b42ecc6daa8][boundary]]. poslib installs
nothing in a product, and it reads and changes nothing in it. Its files
are not part of the [[id:93d0542c-ef28-4af2-bff4-5f16e3f87021][corpus]].
The [[id:a5472c74-3ec5-482d-9e12-de172e55cb31][dependency rule]] does not
apply inside a product.
